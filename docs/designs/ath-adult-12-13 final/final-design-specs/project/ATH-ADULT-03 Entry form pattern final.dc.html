The athlete entry-form pattern

Twenty-two artboards across three forms — wellness, RPE and the weekly nutrition check-in. Heart rate and weight is a disclosure inside wellness, not a form of its own. Nothing is clickable and no hovers or transitions are active. Question wording is the live build's, verbatim. The first seventeen frames are the form screens at their top scroll position; the last five are the bottom-sheet presentation all three forms now open in, with the sheet owning its own scroll area and footer.
