The athlete gym logger pattern

Thirteen artboards covering logging a session set by set, finishing early, and correcting a logged set. The coach prescribes sets, weight and reps; the athlete confirms with one tap or adjusts with steppers first. A set records weight and reps only. Structure is carried by spacing and one accent rather than by borders: white surfaces sit on the tinted page, the two numbers being logged are the largest thing on the screen, and every label around them is quiet. Nothing is clickable and no transitions are active. Session shown is Conor Moroney, Lower A, 12 sets across 4 exercises — the session the three reviews were walked on.
