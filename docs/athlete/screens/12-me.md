# Me

## 1. Where it sits

Tab 4 of 4. Route `/me`. File 270 lines.

## 2. Who reaches it and when

Every athlete.

## 3. What you see

Their name and club, a password change, a data export, links to notifications and
leaderboard settings, and the Apple Health toggle.

## 4. What the athlete enters here

| Field | As worded | Type | Validation | On invalid | Stored | Editable | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current password | UNVERIFIED | text | re-authentication before change | rejected | `auth.users` | n/a | nobody |
| New password | UNVERIFIED | text, at least 12 characters | client and GoTrue | rejected | `auth.users` | n/a | nobody |
| Apple Health toggle | UNVERIFIED | on or off | consent write | n/a | `athlete_consents.purpose = 'healthkit_sync'` | yes, withdrawable | staff can see consent state |

## 5. Every number shown

| Metric ID | Label | Meaning | Window | When missing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MET-005 | Body mass | Their latest weigh in | Latest | empty |

## 6. Every thing you can act on

| Element | Where | What happens | Takes you to | Writes | Confirm | Hidden when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Change password | Body | Re-authenticates, then updates | stays | `auth.users` | yes, current password | never |
| Export my data | Body | Downloads a CSV | `/me/export` | an `audit_log` row | no | never |
| Notifications | Row | Opens settings | `/me/notifications` | nothing | no | never |
| Leaderboards | Row | Opens settings | `/me/leaderboards` | nothing | no | never |
| Apple Health | Toggle | Grants or withdraws consent | stays | `athlete_consents` | UNVERIFIED | **shown only on Premium** (`me/page.tsx:240`) |
| Log out | Bottom | Ends the session | `/login` | nothing | UNVERIFIED | never |
| Report a problem | Row | Opens the form | `/report-problem` | nothing | no | never |

**This is the only tier gate in the entire athlete shell**: `isPremium(tier)`
at `me/page.tsx:240`, guarding the Apple Health toggle.

## 7. Offline and sync

**UNVERIFIED.** Consent changes and notification preferences are not in the
outbox, so they presumably require a connection. DECISION 11.

## 8. Notifications

None open this screen.

## 9. Permissions

**The Apple Health toggle asks for a permission that leads nowhere.** There is no
HealthKit integration: no native code, and no table holds device sourced sleep,
resting heart rate or HRV. The toggle writes a consent row and nothing reads it
to ingest anything. **DECISION 4.**

## 10. States

Loading, Basic club (no Apple Health toggle), error.

## 11. Accessibility and device

percent, screen reader labels, browser support policy.
