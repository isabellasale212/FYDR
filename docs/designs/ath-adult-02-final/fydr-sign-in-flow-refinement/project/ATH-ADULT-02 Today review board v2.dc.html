Confirmed, and changed

The left accent stripe on a card is not the system pattern

I checked, and it is the opposite of the rule. The system states that the tinted border replaces the 3px left bar as the card-level urgency device, and that --border-accent-w 3px is retained for dense list rows only: cards get the tinted fill, rows keep the bar. The availability card is a card, so the stripe I drew on the approved board was wrong. It now takes its fill, border and foreground from one tone family, which is the documented semantic-card treatment.
