Settings, users and club setup

Fourteen artboards: eleven staff desktop at 1440 × 900, three phone at 375 × 812. Artboard 0 is the setup checklist a club lands on the first time it signs in; 1 to 10 are the settings a running club uses. Settings is not one long page. It is four groups — the club, the people, the data, your own account — and every destination inside them uses the same shell: a title, one sentence saying what the screen governs and who it affects, the controls, and the consequence stated before the button that causes it. Destructive and irreversible work (retention, subject access, exports, roles) states scope, count and reversibility in the same sentence as the action. Nothing is clickable and no transitions are active. Signed in as Jane Pemberton (sport scientist, club admin) except artboard 10, which is Ruth Callaghan (medical). Ashcombe Rugby Club, 30 athletes, 7 staff. Board instant: Friday 11 September 2026, 09:12 Europe/London.

THE GAP THIS CLOSES

A row that looks pressable is pressable across all of it

Log out on the running hub is a 742px row whose only live target is a 4.8px chevron. Every row on this board is one control for its full width and height, and anything that ends a session or deletes data is a labelled button, not a row in a list.

SCOPE BEFORE ACTION

Who it touches, how many, and whether it can be undone

Retention names the rows it will delete and the date they fall out. A role change names what the person gains and loses. An export reads its own filters back. The count is computed before the button, never after.

WHAT THIS BOARD REFUSES

No table that scrolls sideways on a phone, no 5,000px hub

At 375 a table becomes a stack of cards carrying the same fields. The hub becomes four groups with counts, not forty rows in sequence. The audit log's filters live in a sheet rather than 6,287px above the entries.

Setting a club up, the hub, and the people who use it

Four groups, each a column, each row saying what it governs rather than repeating its own title. Destinations a role cannot open are not drawn. Roles and invitations follow: the club's seven staff, what each role can see, and the invitation that has not been accepted.
