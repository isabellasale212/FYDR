# Premium: downgrade and restore

**Decided by Isabella, 13 September 2026.**

When a club stops paying for premium, the premium data already in the database
is **kept and hidden**.

- The data stays. The screens and regions that display it disappear, following
  the D-20 rule: a wholly premium destination vanishes from the sidebar and
  refuses at the URL; a premium region inside a base page shows an upsell card
  and never vanishes silently.
- If the club pays again, everything is there. A club that lapses in November
  and returns in March finds its history intact.
- **Kept does not mean kept forever.** The data stays under the club's normal
  retention policy, the same clock that governs everything else they hold.
  Without that condition, premium data becomes a category with no retention
  rule, which is the first thing a solicitor will ask about.

## Rejected

- **Delete after a grace window.** Destroying an athlete's history because an
  administrator missed an invoice is indefensible.
- **Keep and show read-only.** A half-state that mostly functions as a nag, and
  it leaves premium content on screen for a club not paying for it.

## Consequence for S12

S12 is designed as the MECHANISM, not the contents: absence, upsell, downgrade
and restore, keyed to any feature. The premium feature inventory drops into the
mechanism when it lands, without a new board. S12 therefore does not answer
"what do I get for paying"; that is the inventory's job.
