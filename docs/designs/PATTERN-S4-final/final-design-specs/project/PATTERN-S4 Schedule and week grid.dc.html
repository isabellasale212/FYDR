The gap this closes

A session with no group has no expected attendees

The form validated three of its nine inputs. Groups and duration are now required, and the message says the consequence rather than the rule: without a group nobody is expected at the session, so it will not appear on any athlete's Today and no RPE task exists. Rehab, twice on one form

A type has a colour. A group has a size

Rehab is one of the seven session types and one of the four squad groups. Rather than rename either, each row carries the thing only it has: a type chip carries its grid colour, and a group chip carries its athlete count. “Rehab” with an amber bar is the type; “Rehab · 5 athletes” is the group. The two rows are also separated on the form, and each says what it decides.

Why removal can be blunt

Remove is only offered before the session, so no rating can exist

The product already withholds Remove for a session in the past. That one existing rule is what lets the confirmation promise something true: the RPE task leaves the athletes' To do and nobody's rating is touched, because nobody can have rated a session that has not happened. Moving a session that has already been rated is the same question with a different answer, and it is listed under Open against code rather than designed around.

Open against code

Expected attendees must be counted distinctly

Nothing in the captures shows an expected count on a session, and adding group sizes assumes no athlete belongs to two groups. Two decisions are needed before any count is printed: count distinct athletes across the selected groups, and decide whether an athlete marked unavailable — injured, or out on the availability board — is excluded from the expectation and from its RPE task, or stays expected and shows as absent. The board names groups wherever the distinct number is unknown.

Can a past or already-rated session be edited at all?

Remove is withheld for a session in the past; Edit is not shown to be. If a rated session’s time is changed, does the athlete’s rating follow it, detach, or become a revision? The rule that submitted entries are immutable suggests refuse-or-revise. Until that is answered, the popover opens read-only for those sessions with the reason stated, and no rule is invented.

Does the grid accept a drag onto an occupied slot?

A drag saves immediately, so an overlap is written with no confirmation step. Whether two sessions may share a time for the same group — legitimate across different groups, doubtful within one — is unobserved, as is what an athlete’s Today does with two sessions at 09:30.

Can a session in the past, or one already rated, be edited at all?

Remove is withheld for a session in the past; Edit is not shown to be. The popover therefore opens read-only for a past or already-rated session with a line saying why, and no rule is invented about what an edit would do to a rating already given. Needed: whether the API refuses a write to a past session, whether a rating is held against the session id, and whether an edit to a rated session should be refused or should create a revision, as the design system’s immutability rule implies.

Moving a session that has already been rated

Not designed here, deliberately. Three things are unobservable: whether a past session can be edited at all (Remove is withheld, Edit is not shown to be), whether a rating is held against the session id or against a date, and whether changing a rated session's time rewrites what the athlete already answered about. The rule in the design system says submitted entries are immutable and a correction creates a revision, which suggests the move should be refused or should create a revision — but the data cannot confirm either. Needs an answer before the move case is drawn.

Where does “RPE due by 19:45” come from?

The athlete-app preview in the captures shows 19:45 for a session ending 10:45 — nine hours later, so neither a fixed offset from the end nor an obvious end-of-day. Is it a club setting, a fixed local time, or derived? The board prints the captured string and never derives a due time itself.

Does Apply merge or replace, and what does a template row hold?

STAFF-SS-15 shows one template with its offsets, “6 days planned · 8 sessions · load 2,910” and “Never applied”, and nothing about what a session in it carries — name, time, duration, location, groups — or what happens to a week that already has sessions. Artboard 10 assumes merge and counts rows rather than naming them. Also unobserved: where load 2,910 comes from, given the grid reports minutes and not load.

Are the Rehab type and the Rehab group the same entity?

Both exist with that exact label. If a Rehab-type session auto-selects the Rehab group, or if the group list is club-configurable while the type list is fixed, the disambiguation in artboards 7 and 14 should say so rather than relying on colour and count. Also unobserved: whether the four group names are seeded or club-defined.

Fixture is a session type and a separate creation route

“+ Fixture” opens its own form with Opponent, Kick-off, Home or away, Venue, Competition and Importance, and refuses with “Name the opponent.” — while Fixture is also one of the seven session types on the session form. What does a session of type Fixture create, and does it appear on the same grid as a real fixture? STAFF-SS-11 says a fixture is not a session for the purposes of Remove.

What is “Timetable”?

The session panel says “2 athletes have a restriction this session may conflict with. See Timetable for who.” There is no Timetable destination in the nine-item sidebar. The string is kept verbatim on artboards 4 and 12 rather than being pointed somewhere invented; it should name a screen that exists, and the two athletes should be nameable in place.

Expected attendees must be counted distinctly

The board prints 27, not 14 + 15: an athlete in two of a session’s groups is one expected attendee, not two, and the number drives the RPE tasks written, the compliance denominator and the dashboard. Two decisions are needed before it is built. First, the count must be distinct athletes across the picked groups, which means group membership has to be queryable per session. Second, whether an athlete who is unavailable — or whose restriction excludes this session’s type — is excluded from the expectation, or counted and then excused. Until that is settled every count on this board should be read as distinct-across-groups with nobody excluded.

With no queue, is there any undo?

A drag saves immediately, a removal is immediate, and Save changes writes at once. Restore session covers the removal case; nothing covers a mis-drag except dragging it back, and nothing covers a Save changes the author regrets. The board does not invent an undo. If athletes can see a session move twice in a minute, that is the cost of the decision and it should be recorded as accepted rather than discovered.

Does removing a session remove its RPE task, or orphan it?

Artboard 9 promises the task leaves the athletes’ To do. That is the only defensible behaviour, but it is not observable from the schedule captures, and ATH-ADULT-06 shows a “session not found” state for rating a session that no longer resolves — which suggests tasks can outlive their session. If they can, the confirmation's second row is wrong and the task needs explicit cleanup.

Is the chip row present in Read mode for an editor?

“+ Session / + Fixture / Week templates” is captured only with the page in Edit mode, and STAFF-SS-12 says only the toolbar is Edit-only. Artboard 1 keeps the chip row in Read mode on that reading. If the chip row is also Edit-only, Read mode loses three controls and Week templates needs another route.

Are the step and range on the panel enforced anywhere else?

The panel states “Steps 15 minutes, 07:00–21:00” and “Steps 5 minutes, 15–180”. The create form takes a free time and a free duration. The error copy in artboard 8 quotes 15 to 180 for duration on that basis; whether the form shares those bounds, and whether the grid can hold a session outside 07:00–21:00 at all, is unobserved.

Which week is the seed?

The approved STAFF-SS-01 board's week strip reads Mon 7 “Gym, lower A · 16:00 · 24 assigned” and Tue 8 “Pitch, conditioning · 18:30”. The schedule captures for the same week read Mon 7 “Unit skills · 09:30” and Tue 8 “Conditioning · 09:30”. This board follows the schedule captures, since they are the schedule. One of the two seeds is stale, and the dashboard strip and the grid must agree before either is built.

Is the day header's minute figure session length or athlete minutes?

The per-day figures sum exactly to the captured footer total of 445, so both are session length — yet the footer calls them athlete contact minutes and Contact time per group reports 0m for every group on the same week, which cannot be true if the same sessions have groups. Either the group card was empty in the capture's seed or it measures something else. The board relabels the footer and prints per-group minutes from the visible sessions.

Where the data came from

Every field name, label, placeholder, session name, time, duration, day header, MD offset, banner sentence, confirmation sentence, button label and caption on this board is read off the walkthrough captures STAFF-SS-07 to SS-15 and STAFF-NUT-07r, or off the approved STAFF-SS-01 and PATTERN-S3 boards. Nothing about ratings, template contents, RPE due times or distinct attendee counts is read from a capture.
