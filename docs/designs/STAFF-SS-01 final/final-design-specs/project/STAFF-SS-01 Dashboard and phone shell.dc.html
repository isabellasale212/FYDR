The shell decision · §0af

Below 768px the sidebar becomes a bottom bar of four plus More

The nine rows and Log out stop stacking above the content. Dashboard, Squad, Schedule and Flags sit in a fixed bottom bar; the remaining five destinations and Log out live in a More sheet. Content starts at y = 64 instead of y = 2209, and every row in the sheet is 52px. The 214px sidebar at 1024 and up, and the 64px rail between 768 and 1023, are untouched.

The gate, stated once

Every role sees the status and the restriction. Only the medic sees the reason.

Data rule 6 in the system: coaching staff see availability status and a restriction line only. So “Ruled out · Alex Grant (Academic)” loses its bracket for everyone but the medic, and the restriction line — what the athlete may do — stays, because that is what a session is planned against.

What 72 became

Named athletes first, with the evidence. The rest behind one count.

The card leads with the number of athletes who need attention, not the number of flag rows. Each one names the athlete, the reading in plain words and the dates behind it. “70 other open flags” is one row at the bottom of the list, and it is a link, not a headline.
