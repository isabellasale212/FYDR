# Scope: v1

**Decided by Isabella, 13 September 2026.**

The target is **v1**: the complete product across free and premium, ready to
sell. It is not a reduced first-club scope.

"Enough for a pilot" is no longer a valid argument for deferring anything. Any
decision closed on those grounds is reopened and must be re-argued on its own
merits.

## What this changed on the day it was made

- Permission-denied reference codes and the denial log: deferred because there
  was no support desk. **Reversed, build them.** A product that is sold
  acquires a support desk immediately, and a denial a user cannot describe is a
  call that cannot be resolved.
- Subject access handled manually by a staff member: acceptable for one
  friendly club, not across a customer base under a one-month legal deadline.
  **Needs a real answer in the product.**
- The match report's thinness: a graceful fallback was acceptable for a pilot.
  **A product sold to rugby clubs that cannot say who played is not complete.**
- Staff offline (PATTERN-S6 C4 and C5): deferred as post-pilot. **In scope.**

## What it did not change

PATTERN-S6 C9, the persistent live-region architecture, stays deferred. It is
deferred on its own merits: 34 of 37 surfaces already comply at runtime and the
one real violation is fixed.

## The word

Use **v1**, not pilot. A pilot is a customer. v1 is the product.
